Our Process

Four Steps From Vulnerability
to Verified Security

Transparent, structured, and built around your business — not technical jargon.

01

Scoping & Strategy

We take the time to understand your business, your regulatory obligations, and your highest-risk assets. Together, we define a clear scope and rules of engagement tailored to your needs.

02

Active Testing

Our certified ethical hackers safely simulate real attacks on your systems — without disrupting your operations. Every technique is controlled, documented, and authorized.

03

Executive Reporting

You receive a clear, jargon-free report designed for business owners — not just your IT team. Every finding is rated by business impact and paired with a specific, actionable fix.

04

Remediation Support

We don't just hand you a report and disappear. We work alongside your IT team to ensure every vulnerability is patched and verified — giving you documented proof of compliance.
